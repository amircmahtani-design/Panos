SPACING
Two rules in styles.css, not one-off nudges, so every page keeps the same
rhythm:

  .banner + .sec-pad          a section opening straight under a page banner
                              no longer repeats the banner's own bottom space
                              (104px -> 60px, and less again on phones)

  two plain sections in a row share ONE gap instead of stacking one section's
  bottom padding onto the next one's top. That is the 208px between the team
  cards and "Τι λένε οι ασθενείς μας" — now 104px.

  Where the background changes (the pale bands on the homepage and the clinic
  page) the full padding stays, because there the space is what separates the
  two colours. Nothing on those pages moved.
